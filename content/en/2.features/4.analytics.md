title: Traffic Analytics and Business Events
description: Understand PV, UV, sources, environment breakdowns, popular pages, and custom business events.
---

Traffic analytics describes who visited which page in what environment. Business events describe which product-critical actions they completed. Both share visitor, session, and page context.

## PV and UV

- **PV (Page Views):** initial page loads and SPA route changes.
- **UV (Unique Visitors):** estimated unique visitors deduplicated by the browser-local `visitor_id`.

UV is a technical estimate. Clearing storage, private browsing, or changing devices creates a new identifier; multiple people sharing one browser may share one identifier.

## Analysis dimensions

Use time trends, paths, referrers, country or region, device, browser, operating system, screen, language, time zone, and network information to find high-traffic pages and affected environments.

## Business events

```js
window.PulseWatch.track('search_completed', {
    result_bucket: '10_50',
    filters_count: 2
})
```

Choose stable, readable event names and send only categorical data needed for analysis. Never include email addresses, phone numbers, tokens, complete forms, or large text. Event `data` counts toward monthly context-data ingestion.

::tip
Maintain an event dictionary with the name, trigger, fields, and owner. This prevents multiple spellings for the same action.
::
